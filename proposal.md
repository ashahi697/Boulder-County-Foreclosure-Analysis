
# Project Proposal

## Research Question

How much higher or lower are residential foreclosure properties sold for compared to their county-assessed property values in Boulder County?

## Project Description

investigate the differences between foreclosure sale prices and assessed property values in Boulder County.
and understanding whether foreclosed properties are consistently sold below their assessed values or whether this relationship changes depending on broader economic conditions.

I plan to examine foreclosure transactions from multiple years, including 2019–2020 during the COVID-19 period and 2026. I also hope to include another year to identify potential patterns over time.

## Data Sources
 Boulder County foreclosure records Boulder County Assessor property records

## Methodology

I plan to collect approximately 10 residential foreclosure transactions from each selected year and compare their recorded sale prices with the assessed values of those properties.

Using Python and pandas, I will calculate the percentage difference between each property's foreclosure sale price and assessed value.

I will then create visualizations comparing the results across different years.

## Intended Audience

This research is intended for real estate investors and prospective homebuyers who may be interested in understanding the potential financial differences between purchasing foreclosed properties and traditional residential properties.

## Expected Outcome

I hope to identify whether foreclosure transactions tend to occur above or below assessed property values and whether these differences vary across the selected years.
